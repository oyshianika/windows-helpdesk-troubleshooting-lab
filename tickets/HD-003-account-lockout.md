# HD-003 — Account locked (simulation)

**Priority:** Medium | **Impact:** One user | **Status:** Escalated in scenario

**Report:** User cannot sign in to their work account after repeated attempts.

**Triage:** Verify identity using the organisation's approved process. Confirm whether there is a wider authentication outage. Ask for the error wording, never the password or MFA code.

**Scenario findings:** Identity is verified; no general outage is reported. The account is locked under policy.

**Action:** Follow the documented access recovery route or assign to the identity team. Check for repeated lockouts that could indicate cached credentials or suspicious activity. Do not bypass policy.

**Validation:** Identity team restores access under policy; user signs in successfully. Record the approved action without sensitive identifiers.
